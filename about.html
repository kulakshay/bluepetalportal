    Our Story

    Bluepetal began at home, with a paintbrush, a plain clutch, and an idea: that everyday fashion could carry something made by hand, not machine.

    What started as a single hand-painted piece has grown into a small collection of clutches, sarees, and stoles — each one sketched, painted, and finished by us, from start to finish. There's no factory line here. Every motif is planned, every stroke is placed by hand, and every piece takes the time it takes.

    We're a tiny, home-run studio — just one or two of us handling everything from design to delivery. That means every order gets real attention, and every piece you see in our catalogue has actually been painted by someone who cares about how it turns out.

    We started with hand-painted fashion accessories because that's where our craft began, but our plan is to keep growing — new categories, new motifs, and eventually a way for you to shop with us online too. For now, we hope you enjoy browsing our catalogue as much as we enjoyed painting it.
